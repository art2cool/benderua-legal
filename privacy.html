Privacy Policy

BEnderUA Shorts Studio · Effective date: 6 November 2026

BEnderUA Shorts Studio ("the App") is a private desktop tool that the BEnderUA channel team uses to publish short videos to the channel's own accounts. This policy explains what data the App handles when an account is connected.

1. Data the App receives

  • From TikTok, after you sign in and grant permission: an access token and refresh token, your display name and username, and the publishing options TikTok allows for your account (privacy levels, maximum video length) — through the scopes user.info.basic and video.publish.
  • Videos and captions that you choose to publish. These are the channel's own content.

The App does not request or read your followers, messages, contacts, location, viewing history or any other data.

2. How the data is used

Only to show which account is connected and to upload the videos you choose, with the captions and privacy settings you select. The data is not used for advertising, profiling or analytics, and is not sold or shared with anyone.

3. Where the data is stored

Tokens and profile information are stored only on the computer where the App runs, in a local file readable only by the owner of that computer's user account. There is no App server and no cloud database. Videos are sent directly from that computer to TikTok.

4. Retention and deletion

Data is kept until you disconnect the App. To remove it, revoke access in TikTok (Settings → Security and permissions → Manage app permissions) and delete the local token file; nothing else is retained by the App.

5. Third parties

Videos you publish are processed by TikTok under the TikTok Privacy Policy. The App shares no data with any other party.

6. Children

The App is not directed at children and is used only by the channel team.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated; the effective date above shows the latest version.
